Conscious Framework

The 4 Questions to Ask Before Every Purchase

Before tapping buy, run through this quick mental checklist to curb impulse buys.

1️⃣

Do I already own something that works?

The greenest item is always the one that already exists in your cupboard or closet.

2️⃣

Can it be repaired or cleaned?

Prioritize modular items that can be maintained rather than glued disposable goods.

3️⃣

What is the end-of-life plan?

Will it sit in a landfill for centuries, or can it biodegrade or be fully recycled into new materials?

4️⃣

Is the material sustainably sourced?

Look for certified organic cotton (GOTS), FSC certified recycled paper, or 100% recycled metals.
