Ecosteps: Water Conservation

Simple water-saving actions for a better tomorrow

🏠 Home

  • 💧 Fix leaky faucets
  • 🚿 Take shorter showers
  • 🛁 Install low-flow fixtures
  • 🌧️ Collect rainwater
  • 🧊 Use greywater for plants

🍳 Kitchen

  • 🍽️ Run full loads in dishwasher
  • 🔥 Boil only needed water
  • 🧊 Use a bowl to wash veggies

🛁 Bathroom

  • 🚿 Install low-flow showerheads
  • 🪣 Turn off tap while brushing
  • 🧽 Fix toilet leaks

🌱 Garden

  • 🌞 Water in early morning
  • 🌧️ Use drip irrigation
  • 🌿 Mulch to retain moisture

🚗 Travel

  • 🚶‍♀️ Walk or bike short distances
  • 🚙 Wash car less frequently
  • 🚌 Use public transport

🚫 Outlier Tip

🏭 Build a Desalination Plant — While it provides fresh water, it's expensive, energy-intensive, and not practical for everyday conservation.
